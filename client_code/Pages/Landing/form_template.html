From an expert's notes to a face people trust

Four steps, and the expert holds the pen at the one that matters.

  1. Give it a face

    Pick a look, a tone and a voice. Start from a template or a blank page.

  2. Feed it sources

    Paste the expert's notes, guides and FAQs, in English or French.

  3. Draft, then approve

    Each answer is drafted from those sources and approved by the expert, in both languages.

  4. Publish and listen

    Share the link. See what people asked that it couldn't answer, and turn it into the next topic.

Questions

Can the agent make things up?

No. It answers only with topics the expert approved: it matches the question to an approved topic and gives that answer word for word. If nothing fits, it says it doesn't know yet and logs the question for the team.

Does it use an AI model?

Not in this version. Drafting picks the sentences from your sources that fit the question, and matching is done with plain rules. An AI model could draft and match better later, behind the same approval step.

Who are Mireille, Théo and Margaux?

Invented guides with invented experts, made to show the studio. The restaurants they mention are real and described with long-known facts only. Always check opening hours before you go.

Can I use it for something other than restaurants?

Yes. The studio doesn't care what the knowledge is about: a museum's collection, a clinic's patient leaflets, a product manual. Start from a blank page.

Who can see what I make?

Your agents are private to you and the expert you invite until you publish. A published agent can be reached by anyone with its link. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.

Does the voice work everywhere?

The face moves its lips on every device. Reading aloud uses the visitor's own browser voices, so it depends on the device and is off until the visitor turns it on.
